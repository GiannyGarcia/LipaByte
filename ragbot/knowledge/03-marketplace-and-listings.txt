LipaByte Marketplace and Listings
=================================

Marketplace
-----------
The Marketplace is where logged-in students browse available tech devices.
Users can:
- View device cards with photos, names, rates, and location
- Open a listing page for full details
- Search or filter listings when those options are available
- Check the owner's public rating when reviews exist

Listing page information
------------------------
A listing typically shows:
- Item name and description
- Category
- Daily rate (shown in Philippine pesos, for example ₱50/day)
- Pickup location such as Lipa City, Batangas
- Availability status
- Owner profile summary and seller reviews

How to list a device (owner / lender)
-------------------------------------
1. Log in.
2. Open List (List your devices).
3. Enter item details: name, category, description, daily rate, and location.
4. Upload photos.
5. Publish the listing.
6. Manage the listing later from My Activity → Listings.

Borrower vs owner roles
-----------------------
- Borrower / renter: browses devices and sends borrow requests.
- Owner / lender: lists devices, reviews incoming requests, and approves or declines them.
A student can be both a borrower and an owner.

Location focus
--------------
LipaByte focuses on Lipa and Batangas students, including areas such as:
- Lipa City
- Batangas City
- Tanauan
- Nearby campuses such as BatStateU and KLL

Tips for good listings
----------------------
- Use clear photos
- Write an honest description of condition and included accessories
- Set a fair daily rate
- Provide a realistic pickup location
- Keep availability updated through the rental workflow
