LipaByte Device Inquiries
=========================

What Device Inquiries is
------------------------
Device Inquiries is a support request feature inside LipaByte.
Logged-in students can submit help requests about borrowing, listing, accounts, or technical issues.

Where to open it
----------------
1. Log in to LipaByte.
2. Click Inquiries in the top navigation (near Messages and My Activity).
3. Or open inquiries.php after login.

How to submit an inquiry
------------------------
1. Enter your full name and email (often prefilled from your account).
2. Choose a category:
   - Borrow Help
   - Listing Help
   - Account Help
   - Technical Issue
   - Other
3. Write a short subject.
4. Write a clear message (at least a short paragraph of detail).
5. Click Submit inquiry.

Tracking inquiries
------------------
- Submitted inquiries appear under Your inquiries.
- You can filter by status or category.
- You can sort newest first or oldest first.
- You can update status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed).
- You can edit the subject/message.
- You can delete an inquiry if it is no longer needed.

What Inquiries is not
---------------------
- Inquiries is not the same as Messages.
- Messages is private chat between renter and owner for an approved rental.
- Inquiries is for general product/support help requests.
- Inquiries does not replace borrow requests on a listing page.

Important note for the assistant
--------------------------------
Explain how students use Inquiries.
Do not claim you can submit an inquiry for them inside this chat.
If they need help with a specific rental pickup, tell them to use Messages after approval.
