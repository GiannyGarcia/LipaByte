LipaByte Reviews and Ratings
============================

When reviews are allowed
------------------------
Reviews unlock only after a rental is marked Completed.

Who can review whom
-------------------
Reviews are two-way and tied to one completed rental:
- The renter reviews the owner
- The owner reviews the renter
Each person may submit only one review per rental.

What a review contains
----------------------
- Star rating from 1 to 5
- Written comment (required, short feedback)

Where to leave a review
-----------------------
- My Activity: pending review cards appear for completed rentals not yet reviewed
- Messages: review form appears in the completed rental conversation

What happens after a review is submitted
----------------------------------------
1. The review is saved in the database.
2. The reviewed user's average rating (avg_rating) is recalculated.
3. The average rating can appear on listing pages, seller profiles, and account summaries.

Why reviews matter
------------------
Reviews build trust in the student community.
Future borrowers can check an owner's rating before requesting a device.
Owners can also see feedback about borrowers after completed rentals.

Rules and safeguards
--------------------
- Only participants of a completed rental can review that rental.
- Duplicate reviews for the same user and same rental are blocked.
- Rating must be between 1 and 5 stars.
- A short comment is required.

What the assistant cannot do
----------------------------
- Submit a review for the user
- Change someone's rating manually
- Invent review scores or claim a live user's current rating unless that exact information is present in the knowledge base
