LipaByte Navigation and My Activity
===================================

Main navigation (logged in)
---------------------------
- Marketplace: browse and search devices
- List: publish a new device listing
- Messages: chat for approved or completed rentals
- Inquiries: submit and manage support inquiries
- My Activity: track renting and listing activity
- Assistant: dedicated help page for the AI assistant
- About: project and developer information

Marketplace tips
----------------
- Use search to find devices by keywords.
- Use category chips such as Audio, Cameras, GPUs, IoT, Laptops, Mobile Testing, VR/AR.
- Open a listing card to see full details, owner info, and the borrow form.
- Daily rates are shown in Philippine pesos (₱).

List (owner side)
-----------------
- Open List to create a listing.
- Provide item name, category, specifications, location, daily rate, optional weekly rate, condition, and photos.
- First uploaded photo is used as the cover image when available.
- Manage listings and incoming borrow requests from My Activity.

My Activity
-----------
My Activity has two main areas:
1. Renting — borrow requests you sent (Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled, Completed)
2. Listings — devices you own, plus incoming requests to approve, decline, or complete

Common owner actions in My Activity:
- Approve a pending request (opens chat)
- Decline a pending request
- Mark an approved rental as Completed (unlocks reviews)

Common renter actions in My Activity:
- Cancel a pending request when allowed
- Open chat for approved or completed rentals
- Leave a review after completion

Notifications
-------------
- The bell icon shows notifications (approvals, chat, completion, and similar events).
- The messages icon can show unread chat counts.

Assistant vs Inquiries vs Messages
----------------------------------
- Assistant: AI help guide using the knowledge base
- Inquiries: support tickets/requests students submit in the app
- Messages: rental coordination chat between two parties after approval
