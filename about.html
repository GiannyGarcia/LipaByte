About LipaByte

Connecting Batangas students through shared tech

LipaByte is a peer-to-peer rental marketplace built for IT and engineering students who need project hardware — without buying everything new.

What is LipaByte?

LipaByte is a web application that lets students in Lipa City, Batangas City, and nearby areas rent and lend tech equipment — Raspberry Pi kits, laptops, GPUs, smartphones for testing, VR gear, cameras, and more.

Instead of every student buying expensive gear for a single subject or capstone, LipaByte creates a trusted campus network where owners list idle equipment and renters send date-based requests. Owners approve rentals, coordinate pickup, and build reputation through reviews.

Why we built it

Project-based courses often require hardware that students use for only a few weeks. Retail prices create a barrier, especially for lower-year students. At the same time, seniors and hobbyists often have kits sitting unused.

LipaByte bridges that gap: affordable short-term access for renters, optional income for lenders, and less e-waste across the campus community.
